# Business Forward program logo

`business-forward-logo.png` was supplied by the project owner on 2026-09-28 (transparent PNG, black lettering with yellow chevrons). It was resized from 2000 × 841 to 720 × 303 for the header, without other changes, and is shown on the light page background. The asset remains the property of its owner.

The earlier Social Innovation Solutions, Raiffeisen Bank and Orange Business logos were removed from the site at the owner's request.

# Favicon

`/favicon.svg`, `/favicon.ico` and `/apple-touch-icon.png` are an original mark for this study: two overlapping speech bubbles (a nod to the SIS logo) in the site palette, with an AI spark in place of the logo's dots. It is not a copy of the SIS logo.

# Fonts

`fonts/` holds Source Sans 3 (variable, Latin and Latin Extended subsets for Romanian diacritics) from the `@fontsource-variable/source-sans-3` package, self-hosted because the Content-Security-Policy only allows same-origin fonts. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`).
